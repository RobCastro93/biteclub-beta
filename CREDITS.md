## Actual-place matching audit

Original OSM dataset contained no image, wikimedia_commons or place wikidata tags (brand:wikidata identifies the brand, not the specific branch, so it was not used as a restaurant photograph). Commons geosearch completed within 150 metres of every one of the 182 named points with zero API errors. Seven nearby file hits (five unique files) were inspected: a gas utility office, a car/street scene, two eclipse views and an unidentified patio. None clearly identified a matching restaurant. Therefore ZERO photographs are represented as actual restaurant photographs. See commons-geosearch-v8.json and commons-local-candidates-v8.json for the audit. Brand-level Wikidata images were not substituted for specific branches.
